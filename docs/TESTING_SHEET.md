# User testing (do this before submitting; it is your strongest evidence)

Test with 15 to 20 people: classmates, family, neighbours. Include some who are not comfortable with technology.

For each person, note: language used, voice or buttons, time to first result, whether they found a scheme they did not
know about, and anything that confused them. Ask them to press 👍 or 👎 on the results page.

The site keeps anonymous totals. Open `https://<your-backend>.onrender.com/feedback/summary` to read:
`total`, `helpful`, `helpful_pct`, `avg_schemes_found`. Write these numbers down before any redeploy.

Pitch lines to prepare from real results (use only true numbers):
- "N people tested it; X% found it helpful."
- "On average it found Y schemes per person."
- "Z people discovered a scheme they had not heard of."
